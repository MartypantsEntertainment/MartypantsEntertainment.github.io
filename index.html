MEET THE TEAM

NASHMIA

party leader

Nashmia brings in over 8 years of experience in the industry. She wears all sorts of hats as the team leader and founder. She fondly misses the golden age of games before AI took over, and yearns to go back to making games the way they were meant to be made.

JOE

lore whiz

Writing and design genius, Joe has decades of experience as a product lead in the real world. Now, he writes impeccable lore for MartyPants, designs puzzles to be pieced togther and let's his feline overlords rule over him in his free time.

EMILY

scare genius

If you want to know what makes horror games tick, you ask Emily. With her degree in English Literature and an eye for detail, she dissects games to find the core that makes them work. NOTHING fazes her, mind you. She plays the scariest games without even flinching.
